How does satellite internet technology work?

This is how satellite internet works:

  1. When you click on your favorite website using your laptop or mobile device, your request goes to a modem, then through a short cable to the dish outside your home.
  2. The dish installed at your home beams your request up to a satellite.
  3. The satellite sends your website request down to a gateway station connected to a ground network.
  4. The station then relays your request to your favorite website.
  5. The website delivers the information you requested back to the station, which sends it up to the satellite to be sent directly to you.

Who is Viasat best for?

Viasat Internet is a good option for households in rural or remote areas where cable and fiber don't reach. It works well for essential, everyday online activities like shopping, banking, education, and staying in touch with family, as well as streaming, video calls, remote work, browsing, and connecting multiple devices.

What activities are not ideal with satellite internet?

Satellite internet works well for most everyday online tasks. Where it can show limitations is with activities like fast-paced multiplayer games that require very low latency, as well as VPNs.

However, for most households, including those who stream and work remotely, it's a dependable connection.
