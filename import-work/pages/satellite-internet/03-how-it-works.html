How satellite internet works

Satellite internet is a type of connection that delivers internet access wirelessly. It uses signals between a satellite in orbit and a dish installed at a home. Unlike cable or fiber, it doesn’t rely on physical wires to reach the customer's home.

Satellite internet works by sending data from your connected device to your home dish, which transmits the signal to a satellite in space. The satellite sends data to ground stations linked to the internet. The response comes back the same way. This enables connectivity, even in remote areas.
